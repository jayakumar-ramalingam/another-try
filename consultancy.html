Case Study 01 · 2012-2019 · TCS

Home Depot: international merchandising transformation

Legacy applications, spreadsheets, sequential approvals and scheduled processing connected item and SKU maintenance, assortment, planogram placement, inventory, pricing and store execution. Some pricing changes took more than a week to reach stores.

Consultancy judgement

I evaluated optimisation of the existing monolith, wholesale replacement, and progressive separation of business capabilities. I recommended phased modernisation to deliver value incrementally while controlling operational risk.

Contribution and impact

  • Advised merchant, product, and engineering stakeholders.
  • Provided technical delivery leadership to an onsite and offshore group of more than twenty engineers, while priorities and final approvals remained with Home Depot stakeholders.
  • Defined service boundaries, data flows, and migration sequencing.
  • Delivered item, SKU, assortment, planogram, inventory, pricing, and markdown capabilities.
  • Introduced event-driven pricing and on-demand store execution.
  • Reduced pricing propagation from more than one week to minutes.

Case Study 02 · 2019-2021 · Mastech Digital

Manheim, Cox Automotive: vehicle-data modernisation

Business context

Manheim operates a large wholesale vehicle marketplace supported by high-volume vehicle, valuation, build, condition, and history information.

Consultancy outcome

I assessed the vehicle-data service architecture, advised on modernisation and service decomposition, and guided reliable high-throughput processing. After the work demonstrated value, Manheim extended my remit to vehicle-report generation.

Demonstrated Outcomes

Transformation results across both engagements.

At Home Depot, the programme established integrated merchandising capabilities spanning item and SKU maintenance, pricing and store execution. Online prices could be updated in real time and store label-print requests dispatched on demand. Changes that previously required more than a week could reach customers in minutes. Improved pricing, markdown and waste visibility contributed to savings measured in millions.

At Manheim, successful delivery of the vehicle-data work resulted in my remit being extended to vehicle-report generation.
