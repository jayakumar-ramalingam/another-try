About / Jayakumar Ramalingam

An architect shaped by production experience.

I’m a Staff Software Engineer at SiriusXM, working on cloud architecture, personalisation and the services that connect data and machine learning to the listening experience.

Over more than sixteen years, I’ve built and modernised systems across media, retail, automotive and financial services. My work spans hands-on engineering, architecture and technical delivery with teams in India and the United States.

Industry work

Building systems people depend on.

2021 to present

SiriusXM

Staff Software Engineer

I led the architecture and technical delivery of the personalisation platform built for SiriusXM’s next-generation streaming service. The engineering team and I developed services for listener preferences, collections and libraries, behavioural events, notifications, recommendations and ranking integration, including the final “For You” response.

The architecture combines batch and machine-learning outputs with recent listening context. I also designed and wrote a common caching library reused by several SiriusXM teams, and documented architectural decisions in a shared repository so other engineers could apply the reasoning to their own systems.

2010 to 2021

TCS & Mastech Digital

Technology consultancy

My consultancy career included engagements with Morgan Stanley, Home Depot and Manheim, a Cox Automotive business. At Home Depot, I worked offshore from 2012 to 2015 and onsite from 2015 to 2019, advising stakeholders and leading technical delivery across merchandising, pricing, item and SKU management, planogram placement and inventory capabilities.

At Manheim, through Mastech Digital, I worked on vehicle-data services and vehicle-report generation. These engagements taught me to work within a client’s decision process: evaluate options, explain the trade-offs and lead delivery against the direction agreed with business and engineering stakeholders.

How I work

Make the reasoning reusable.

I use architecture reviews to work through assumptions, failure modes and operational consequences with engineers. The decisions go into shared records so teams can understand why an approach was chosen and when it might need to change.

I also mentor engineers on system design, cloud architecture and progression into senior responsibilities, both at work and through ADPList.
